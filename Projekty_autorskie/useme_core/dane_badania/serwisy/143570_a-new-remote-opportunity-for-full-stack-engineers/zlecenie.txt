We’re expanding our engineering team and looking for talented Mid–Senior Full Stack Engineers who are interested in building great products, working with modern technologies, and becoming part of a long-term international team.

Wymagane funkcje:
We’re interested in engineers who have:

Mid–Senior level full-stack development experience
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
Good understanding of modern software development practices
C1 English or higher, with confident written and spoken communication
Reliable, high-speed internet and a professional remote setup
A proactive attitude and strong sense of ownership
The ability to communicate clearly and work independently

I am looking for a custom, original solution rather than a ready-made platform. The project should be scalable, secure, user-friendly, and optimized for performance. I am open to discussing the best technology stack and architecture based on the project requirements.
