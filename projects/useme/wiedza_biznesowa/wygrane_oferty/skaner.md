# Request for Quotation — Airtable consultancy

## About us

We are a rental management company based in Krakow, managing apartments on behalf of property owners (short-term and long-term rentals). Our knowledge base, operational procedures, and data are currently kept in Google Drive and Google Sheets, and we are migrating to Airtable to support our operations at scale.

## Scope of work

We are looking for an Airtable consultant to help us with the following:

Data migration from Google Sheets to Airtable — including restructuring of the source data into a clean, normalized schema suitable for ongoing operational use.

Integration with external booking systems — automate the pulling of data from third-party booking platforms into Airtable.

Operational workflow tools built in Airtable - maintenance tracking, cleaning schedule...
